Detail level: STANDARD.
- Open with a two- or three-sentence bottom line that answers the question
  directly.
- Then cover the supporting detail properly: the sequence of events with
  their dates, each party's position and its reasoning, and the figures,
  deadlines and conditions the sources give. Aim for roughly 500–800
  words; go shorter only when the record is thin, and say that it is.
- Use short bold lead-ins ("**The order:**", "**NEPGA's protest:**") or
  bullets where they help a reader navigate; otherwise short paragraphs.
  No headings.
- Close with a sentence or two on what remains unresolved or comes next,
  with dates where the record gives them.
